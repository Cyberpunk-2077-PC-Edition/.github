## Game Features and Systems

### Night City and Open-World Exploration

Cyberpunk 2077 takes place in Night City, a futuristic metropolis shaped by corporate power, advanced technology, street gangs, and social inequality. Its districts feature distinct architecture, local identities, commercial spaces, and environmental details that contribute to the game's setting.

Exploration extends beyond the main roads and major landmarks. Players can discover smaller streets, hidden locations, shops, mission entrances, and optional encounters while travelling between objectives. The city serves as more than a backdrop: its inhabitants, public spaces, and local conflicts provide context for many of the game's activities.

The surrounding Badlands offer a different environment from the densely populated city centre. These areas introduce additional locations, travel routes, and encounters that broaden the exploration experience.

### Story Campaign and Player Decisions

The main campaign follows V, a mercenary whose life changes after a dangerous operation involving experimental technology. The resulting story combines personal survival with corporate conflicts, relationships, and questions about identity.

Conversations and mission decisions can influence character relationships, subsequent dialogue, and certain story outcomes. Some choices affect later events or determine which activities become available. Paying attention to character motivations and mission circumstances can therefore make a difference to the experience.

The narrative combines scripted story sequences with opportunities to explore and complete optional activities between major missions. This structure allows players to balance the central storyline with the wider world.

### Main Missions and Side Jobs

The quest system combines primary story missions with optional jobs, contracts, and character-focused narratives. Main missions advance V's central story, while side jobs introduce additional characters, local conflicts, and situations that expand the world.

Optional activities can provide equipment, currency, experience, and additional information about Night City. Some missions are connected through character relationships or earlier events, creating storylines that develop over multiple encounters.

The journal and map help organize objectives, locations, and available activities. Players can use these tools to prioritize the main campaign, pursue optional stories, or explore a particular district.

### Character Attributes and Skill Development

Character development allows players to shape V around different combat and exploration strategies. Attributes and skill categories influence available abilities, equipment options, and effectiveness in selected situations.

A build can emphasize direct confrontation, mobility, technical capabilities, stealth, or a combination of approaches. Choosing abilities according to a preferred play style helps create a coherent character while leaving room to experiment with different tactics.

The character interface provides access to attributes, perks, equipment, and other development systems. Reviewing these options regularly can help players adapt their build as new challenges and equipment become available.

### Cyberware and Technological Enhancements

Cyberware represents the advanced technology integrated into characters throughout the game's setting. Available enhancements can affect mobility, combat capabilities, defensive options, and interactions with the environment.

Different combinations support different character builds. Players can compare the effects of available implants, review their requirements, and select upgrades that complement their chosen skills and equipment.

Cyberware management is particularly relevant when adapting a character to more demanding encounters. The right combination can support a specific approach without requiring every character build to follow the same strategy.

### Combat, Hacking, and Stealth

Cyberpunk 2077 offers several approaches to hostile encounters. Players can engage enemies directly, seek advantageous positions, avoid detection, or use technical abilities to influence the situation.

Hacking provides tactical options for interacting with compatible systems and targets. Depending on the abilities available, players can use these tools to create opportunities, disrupt opponents, or support a more controlled approach to combat.

Stealth-oriented builds emphasize concealment, careful movement, and target selection. Combat-focused builds can instead prioritize weapon handling, defensive capabilities, and direct engagement. Combining these systems allows players to adapt to different mission layouts and objectives.

### Weapons, Equipment, and Inventory

Equipment selection plays an important role in character development. Different weapon categories support different combat styles, while item characteristics and character abilities influence their usefulness in specific situations.

The inventory interface helps players review collected equipment, compare available items, and manage resources. Weapons, clothing, consumables, and cyberware can be evaluated according to their practical benefits and compatibility with a character build.

As the campaign advances, players can refine their equipment choices around preferred tactics. Maintaining a suitable selection of weapons and useful items helps prepare for encounters with different layouts and enemy types.

### Vehicles and Transportation

Night City includes a range of cars and motorcycles that provide different ways to travel between districts. Vehicles can be used to reach quest locations, explore the city, and move through the environment according to the current activity.

Driving characteristics and handling vary between vehicle models. Players can select transportation based on appearance, acceleration, handling preferences, and practical travel needs.

Vehicle access also contributes to exploration and mission preparation. Travelling through different parts of the city reveals additional locations and helps connect activities across the open world.

### Factions, Characters, and Relationships

Night City contains corporations, gangs, fixers, independent operators, and other groups with competing interests. Interactions with these characters establish the social and political tensions that shape the setting.

Character-focused quests provide background information and can introduce decisions that influence relationships or later events. These storylines complement the central campaign by presenting different perspectives on life in the city.

The result is a world where optional activities contribute to its atmosphere and characterization, rather than serving only as additional objectives.

### Phantom Liberty Expansion

Phantom Liberty is a story expansion that introduces a spy-thriller narrative, the Dogtown district, new characters, additional missions, and further gameplay content. Its story brings V into a conflict involving intelligence operations, political interests, and dangerous alliances.

The expansion adds activities, equipment, cyberware, and a dedicated progression branch associated with the Relic system. It provides another substantial narrative experience for players who own the required content.

Phantom Liberty requires the base game. Its availability and included content depend on the edition purchased, so players should review the relevant store listing before choosing an edition or buying the expansion separately.

### Graphics, Lighting, and Audio

Cyberpunk 2077 uses detailed city environments, lighting effects, atmospheric rendering, character animation, and a varied soundtrack to establish its futuristic setting. Visual presentation changes between dense streets, commercial interiors, residential areas, and locations outside the city centre.

Available graphics settings allow players to adjust image quality according to their hardware. Advanced lighting features can increase visual detail but also place additional demands on the graphics processor.

Audio, dialogue, environmental sound, and music contribute to the atmosphere of missions and exploration. Together, these elements help distinguish districts and reinforce the mood of individual story sequences.

### Interface, Controls, and Accessibility

The game provides settings for display, audio, controls, subtitles, and supported accessibility features. Players can adjust available input bindings and interface options to suit their preferred configuration.

The interface organizes quests, character attributes, equipment, map information, and inventory management. Learning these menus makes it easier to review objectives, prepare for missions, and compare available upgrades.

Display settings and input options can also help establish a comfortable setup before starting longer sessions. Available features vary by platform and game configuration.
